ILPA Model Limited Partnership Agreement
=========================================
Source: https://ilpa.org/industry-guidance/templates-standards-model-documents/model-limited-partnership-agreement/
Fetched: 2026-03-28

OVERVIEW
--------
The Institutional Limited Partners Association created standardized Model Limited Partnership
Agreements (LPAs) to address persistent needs in private equity. These documents aim to reduce
negotiation complexity, lower legal costs, and provide fair terms for both General Partners (GPs)
and Limited Partners (LPs).

"A standard Model Limited Partnership Agreement ('LPA') has been a persistent need in the private
equity asset class given the cost, time and complexity of negotiating the terms of investment."

The models help GPs achieve fundraising certainty and lower formation costs while enabling LPs to
understand rights and obligations with reduced legal expenses.

DOCUMENT VERSIONS
-----------------
ILPA released two comprehensive, Delaware law-based model LPAs:

1. Whole of Fund Model - Originally released October 2019; revised July 2020
2. Deal by Deal Model - Released July 2020

Both accommodate traditional private equity buyout fund structures with different distribution
waterfall arrangements.

DEVELOPMENT HISTORY
-------------------
The Model LPA project began in early 2018, involving approximately 20 attorneys representing
both GPs and LPs globally. Development included extensive drafting and negotiation processes.
